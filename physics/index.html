How to approach it
  1. Sketch the physical situation, then choose axes.
  2. Write down every given quantity and its units.
  3. Circle what the question asks for.
  4. Choose a formula that contains those quantities.
  5. Rearrange the formula before plugging in numbers.
  6. Check the units and whether the answer makes physical sense.
