A few dotfiles worth stealing

Not a full dotfiles dump — just the handful of changes that made the biggest difference relative to how small they are. All three are in the downloads section if you want the real files instead of the summary.

A dedicated scratchpad terminal

One Hyprland keybind that spawns a floating, centered terminal on any workspace. It's the single most-used shortcut on my machine — quicker than switching workspaces for anything that takes under a minute.

A Waybar module that shows nothing, on purpose

I removed CPU, RAM and disk usage from my bar entirely. I never once made a decision based on glancing at them, and an empty corner of the screen turned out to be worth more than the data was.

A starship prompt that only shows git status when it's dirty

A clean repo gets a clean prompt. The moment something's staged, modified, or ahead of origin, the prompt grows a section for it. Saves a full line of vertical space most of the time, without hiding anything I actually need.
